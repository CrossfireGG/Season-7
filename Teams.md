## Seeding Notes

### VRS Seeding Priority

- **All teams enter through fully open registration** — no teams are invited. Valve Regional Standings (VRS) is applied afterward, purely as a seeding input, to preserve VRS integrity for teams that already qualified by signing up
- Teams matching a Valve Regional Standings (VRS) Americas roster are seeded **first**, ordered by VRS standing (best to worst)
- VRS status requires a **core of 3+ shared players** between a team's signed-up Season 7 **starting roster** (Starters only — substitutes are not counted toward this core) and a single VRS roster entry — organization/branding name is not used for matching, since orgs can rebrand between seasons
- Where a team's registered name differs from the VRS org name it matched (roster overlap confirmed), the VRS org name will be noted above — the *players*, not the name, establish VRS status
- The **top 8 VRS-seeded teams** receive a bye directly to Playoffs (see Playoff Bye Teams above) and do not play in the Group Stage — this is a seeding outcome of open registration, not a separate invite

### Non-VRS Teams

- Remaining teams are seeded by **ESEA division tier**, best to worst: **Advanced > Main > Intermediate > Open (Group 9-10) > Open (Group 1-8)**, then by regular-season placement (best to worst) within each tier
- **Fresh Teams** — teams with no VRS match and no qualifying ESEA record — are seeded last, below every ranked team, in a fixed draw order

### Roster & Core Policy (see README.md for full detail)

- A team's Core-3 is the three players submitted in its official starting lineup at registration
- **New for Season 7:** substitute changes are permitted during Playoffs (Core-3 still cannot change)
- VRS teams must keep their matched VRS core-3 present at all times, including through Playoffs

### Tournament Lock Notice

To ensure **fair play and competitive integrity** for all participants, seeding will be finalized prior to Round 1, after which no further seeding or team changes will be made.

**Seeding Method:**

- Seeds 1-8 (all VRS-seeded) bye directly to Playoffs — not placed in a group
- Remaining seeds are distributed across the 12 groups using a snake-seeding pattern in 4 rounds of 12 (Group A→L, then L→A, then A→L, then L→A), so each group gets one team from each seeding band

This ensures balanced groups with mixed skill levels.

## Team Requirements

### Roster Rules

- **Maximum roster:** 8 players (5 starters, 2 substitutes, 1 coach)
- **Minimum core players:** 3 original core players must play per match
- **Roster changes:** Allowed during Group Stage; substitute changes now also allowed during Playoffs (Core-3 locked once Playoffs begin — see README.md)
- **All players must reside in North America**

### Eligibility

- ✅ FACEIT Anti-Cheat required
- ✅ No active VAC or platform bans
- ✅ Team captain designated from core players
- ✅ Entry fee: $200 per team
- ✅ Payment due immediately after registration submission
