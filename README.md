## Tournament Classification

CrossfireGG operates as an open-registration Tier-2 structured competition. **Season 7 is run in alignment with Valve Regional Standings (VRS) integrity guidance.** Valve Regional Standings (VRS) are referenced for seeding integrity and competitive structure. ## Roster & Core Policy

CrossfireGG does not enforce a full roster lock during the regular season or group stage. Teams may adjust players prior to playoffs, subject to tournament administration approval.

All teams must maintain a consistent Team Core (3 players) for competitive integrity. **A team's Core-3 identity is defined by the three players submitted in its official starting lineup at registration.**

### VRS Teams

- Must maintain at least three (3) players from the matched VRS roster (snapshot date October 5th, 2026 — see Timing below) **at all times, including throughout the Playoffs**
- Changing one or two players does not affect VRS eligibility
- Falling below three snapshot players results in immediate disqualification to preserve VRS integrity
- This requirement is unaffected by the Season 7 Playoff substitution allowance below — VRS core presence is mandatory regardless of when a substitution is made

### Non-VRS Teams

- Must also maintain a consistent Core-3 identity for competitive integrity
- Administrative review may occur if the core identity changes

### Duplicate Core Restrictions

- **Duplicate core rosters are not allowed** — no two teams may share 3 or more core players
- Teams found in violation will be disqualified
- This rule ensures roster integrity across the competitive landscape

### Roster Changes & Substitutions

- **Group Stage:** Starters and substitutes may be adjusted, subject to administration approval
- **Playoffs (New for Season 7):** Substitute changes are now permitted during the Playoffs — this was locked in prior seasons. **Core-3 identity may not change during Playoffs**, and for VRS teams, the matched VRS core-3 must remain present regardless of any substitute changes made

### Coach Substitution Rule

- Registered team coaches are permitted to substitute into matches as players, provided they are listed on the official roster prior to match start and meet all eligibility requirements.

## Tournament Format

### Group Stage

- **Format:** Round-Robin Group Stage
- **Structure:** 12 Groups × 4 Teams (48 teams total; the 8 highest-seeded VRS teams bye directly to Playoffs and are not placed in a group)
- **Match Type:** All matches are Best-of-3 (BO3)
- **Duration:** November 2 – November 10, 2026
- **Advancement:** Top 2 teams from each group advance to Playoffs (24 qualifiers), joining the 8 bye teams for a 32-team Playoff field
- **Default Match Day:** The middle day of each round window
- Teams may reschedule matches within their assigned round window with mutual agreement

**Match Rounds:**

- Round 1: November 2 – November 4
- Round 2: November 5 – November 7
- Round 3: November 8 – November 10

**Seeding Break:** November 11 – November 13

### Seeding Method

Teams are seeded using a **VRS-first, record-second methodology**:

1. **VRS-matched teams** — a team is matched to a Valve Regional Standings (Americas) roster when 3 or more of its signed-up **Starters** (substitutes are not counted) also appear on that VRS entry's roster. VRS-matched teams are seeded ahead of all others, ordered by VRS standing (best to worst).
2. **Top 8 seeds bye directly to Playoffs.** The 8 best VRS-matched teams do not play the Group Stage.
3. **Remaining teams are seeded by ESEA League Division**, best to worst: Advanced > Main > Intermediate > Open (Group 9-10) > Open (Group 1-8), then by regular-season record/placement within each tier.
4. **Fresh Teams** — teams with no VRS match and no qualifying ESEA record — are seeded last, below every ranked team.
5. Remaining teams (the non-bye VRS teams + all ESEA-tier + Fresh teams) are distributed across the 12 groups using a snake-seeding pattern, so every group draws from a mix of seeding bands.

Full team-by-team seeding will be published in [Teams and Rankings](Teams.md) once signups close and VRS matching is complete.

### Playoff Stage

- **Format:** 32-Team Double Elimination Bracket
- **Field:** 24 teams qualify through the Group Stage (top 2 per group) + 8 top-seeded teams bye directly in
- **Match Type:** All matches are Best-of-3 (BO3)
- **Grand Final:** Best-of-5 (BO5) — the Winner's Bracket Champion begins the Grand Final with a 1-0 map advantage
- **Duration:** November 14 – December 13, 2026
- **Default Match Day:** The middle day of each playoff window
- Matches may be rescheduled within each round window with admin approval

**Playoff Rounds:**

- WB Round of 32: November 14 – November 16
- WB Round of 16 + Losers Round 1: November 17 – November 19
- WB Quarterfinals + Losers Round 2: November 20 – November 22
WB Quarterfinals may be rescheduled through November 25.
- Losers Round 3: November 23 – November 25
- WB Semifinals + Losers Round 4: November 26 – November 28
WB Semifinals may be rescheduled through December 1.
- Losers Round 5: November 29 – December 1
- WB Final + Losers Round 6: December 2 – December 4
WB Final may be rescheduled through December 7.
- Losers Round 7: December 5 – December 7
- Losers Final: December 8 – December 10
- Grand Final (BO5): December 11 – December 13

## Season 7 vs Season 6 — Key Changes

Format, entry fee, prize pool, and match structure are **unchanged from Season 6**. The changes this season are focused on roster flexibility:

### Roster & Substitution Policy

- **New for Season 7 — Substitutes allowed during Playoffs:** Season 6 fully locked rosters for Playoffs. Season 7 allows substitute changes during Playoffs; Core-3 identity still cannot change once Playoffs begin.
- **New for Season 7 — Core-3 defined at lineup submission:** A team's Core-3 is explicitly defined as the three players submitted in its official starting lineup at registration, closing an ambiguity from Season 6.
- **Unchanged — VRS core presence:** VRS-matched teams must keep their matched VRS core-3 present at all times, including through Playoffs, regardless of the new substitute allowance.

### Schedule

- **Season 6:** Group Stage began August 31, 2026; season ran ~6 weeks.
- **Season 7:** Group Stage begins November 2, 2026; season again runs ~6 weeks (November 2 – December 13). Signups run October 16–30, with seeding and VRS lookup published October 30 – November 1.
