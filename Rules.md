## 1. Registration Regulations

### 1.1 Registration Payment/Fee

* 1.1.1 All teams must register through the official Crossfire registration form.
* 1.1.2 Crossfire GG Season 7 is a VRS-aligned event. Eligible North American teams may register until slots are filled.
* 1.1.3 Entry fee is **$200 per team**. Entry fees are **non-refundable** for any reason unless otherwise stated by Crossfire administration.
* 1.1.4 Payment is **due immediately after registration submission** via the official payment method. Teams failing to pay the entry fee may be removed from the tournament.
* 1.1.5 Upon registration completion, ownership of the team slot is assigned to the designated team captain.

### 1.2 Roster Submission Guidelines

* 1.2.1 During registration, teams must submit five starting players and designate a team captain from that starting lineup.
  * 1.2.1.1 **Five-player reference roster:** For Non-VRS teams, this consists of their five original registered starters. For VRS-matched teams, this consists of the five players listed on their matched VRS roster in the **October 5, 2026 publication**. Teams must field at least three players from their applicable reference roster for the entirety of every match, including Playoffs. The qualifying three may differ between matches; teams do not designate three fixed core players.
  * 1.2.1.2 Team captains may request a captain role transfer at any time, but the new captain must be a registered player on the team's official roster.
* 1.2.2 Teams must provide complete roster information including all player names, Steam IDs, and FACEIT profiles.
* 1.2.3 All players must meet eligibility requirements:
  * Must reside in North America.
  * Must not have active VAC bans or platform bans.
  * Must have FACEIT Anti-Cheat installed.

### 1.3 Roster Limitations

* 1.3.1 Maximum roster size: **8 players total** (5 starters, 2 substitutes, 1 coach).
* 1.3.2 **Non-VRS teams** must field at least three of their five original registered starters. **VRS-matched teams** must field at least three of the five players listed on their matched VRS roster in the **October 5, 2026 publication**. This requirement applies for the entirety of every match, including Playoffs.
* 1.3.3 **Team coaches are permitted to substitute** as players, provided they are listed on the official roster prior to match start and meet all eligibility requirements.
* 1.3.4 **No multi-accounting** is allowed. Violations result in immediate disqualification.
* 1.3.5 **Roster changes:**
  * **Group Stage:** Allowed at any time, subject to admin approval. Roster changes do not alter the applicable five-player reference roster defined in Section 1.2.1.1.
  * **Playoff Stage:** Teams may make a maximum of **2 substitute roster changes total per team** across the Playoffs, limited to the **Sub 1 / Sub 2 slots** and subject to admin approval. Each replacement of one player in either substitute slot counts as one change. Replacing both substitute players counts as two changes.
  * Using a substitute who is already registered on the official roster **does not count as a substitute roster change**.
  * The applicable five-player reference roster remains fixed throughout the tournament. Any three of those five players may satisfy the match requirement; the same three are not required across matches.
  * **VRS-matched teams** must maintain at least three players from their matched **October 5, 2026 VRS roster** on their official tournament roster throughout the tournament and field at least three of those five players for the entirety of every match. Falling below three snapshot players on the official roster results in disqualification. Approved roster changes and substitutions do not waive these requirements.

### 1.4 Substitutions

* 1.4.1 **Between matches:** Teams may use registered substitutes for any reason, provided the lineup fields at least three players from the applicable five-player reference roster defined in Section 1.2.1.1. Using an already registered substitute **does not count as a roster change**. During Playoffs, teams may make a maximum of **2 changes total to the Sub 1 / Sub 2 roster slots**, subject to admin approval; see Section 1.3.5.
* 1.4.2 **During a match or between maps:** Substitutions are permitted only in the event of a **verifiable emergency**, subject to admin approval.
* 1.4.3 Teams must contact an admin immediately via Discord ticket for emergency substitutions.

## 2. Tournament Format and Structure

### 2.1 Tournament Overview

* 2.1.1 **Tournament Type:** Tier 2, VRS-aligned, structured to maintain VRS competitive integrity.
* 2.1.2 **Total Teams:** Up to 56 (48 Group Stage + 8 top-seeded teams receiving a Playoff bye, all via open registration, not invitation).
* 2.1.3 **Prize Pool:** $10,000 USD.
* 2.1.4 **Platform:** All matches hosted on FACEIT.
* 2.1.5 **Server Region:** North America (United States).

### 2.2 Group Stage

* 2.2.1 **Format:** 12 Groups × 4 Teams (48 teams in group stage).
* 2.2.2 **Match Type:** Round-Robin, Best-of-3 (BO3).
* 2.2.3 **Duration:** November 2 – November 10, 2026.
* 2.2.4 **Rounds:**
  * Round 1: Nov 2 – Nov 4 (Default: middle day @ 8 PM EST).
  * Round 2: Nov 5 – Nov 7 (Default: middle day @ 8 PM EST).
  * Round 3: Nov 8 – Nov 10 (Default: middle day @ 8 PM EST).
* 2.2.5 **Advancement:** Top 2 teams from each group advance to Playoffs (24 teams).
* 2.2.6 Teams may reschedule matches within the round window with mutual agreement.
* 2.2.7 **Tiebreakers:** Head-to-Head result between tied teams is the **primary tiebreaker**, followed by round differential / other standard metrics.

### 2.3 Playoff Stage

* 2.3.1 **Format:** 32-Team Double Elimination Bracket.
* 2.3.2 **Composition:** 24 teams qualified from Group Stage + 8 top-seeded teams that received a Playoff bye.
* 2.3.3 **Match Type:** Best-of-3 (BO3) for all playoff matches, Grand Final Best-of-5 (BO5).
* 2.3.4 **Duration:** November 14 – December 13, 2026.
* 2.3.5 **Winner's Bracket Advantage:** Winner's Bracket champion begins Grand Final with a 1-0 map advantage.
* 2.3.6 Reschedules permitted within each playoff round window with admin approval.
* 2.3.7 **Roster note:** Teams may make a maximum of **2 substitute roster changes total** during Playoffs, limited to the **Sub 1 / Sub 2 slots** and subject to admin approval; see Sections 1.3.5 and 1.4.1. Using an already registered substitute does not count toward this limit. Teams must field at least three players from their applicable fixed five-player reference roster for the entirety of every match.
* 2.3.8 **Extended Reschedule Windows:** To protect bracket integrity, the following Winner's Bracket rounds may additionally be rescheduled past their normal round window, with admin approval:
  * WB Quarterfinals may be rescheduled through November 25.
  * WB Semifinals may be rescheduled through December 1.
  * WB Final may be rescheduled through December 7.

### 2.4 Seeding and VRS

* 2.4.1 Seeding will incorporate Valve Regional Standings (VRS) as a priority for VRS-eligible teams, using the fixed VRS publication dated **October 5, 2026**.
  * 2.4.1.1 A team is VRS-matched when at least three of its five registered starters appear on the same five-player roster in the **October 5, 2026 VRS publication**. Substitutes are not counted for initial VRS matching. That matched five-player VRS roster becomes the team's fixed reference roster for the match requirements in Section 1.2.1.1.
* 2.4.2 **Seeding Priority:**
  1. Teams with VRS ranking seeded first by VRS rank.
  2. Remaining teams seeded by their North American ESEA League division in the following order: **ECL > Advanced > Main > Intermediate > Open9-10 > Open1-8 > New Team**.
  3. Within the same division, teams will be ordered by their league win/loss record. Where teams have played different numbers of matches, win percentage will be used.
  4. Ties or teams without a comparable league record will be evaluated using documented competitive results. The basis for any such seeding decision will be published with the final seeding list.
* 2.4.3 Final seeding list will be publicly posted before Group Stage Round 1 begins.

## 3. Online Match Rules

### 3.1 Platform Requirements

* 3.1.1 All matches and servers hosted on **FACEIT platform**.
* 3.1.2 All players required to have a **FACEIT Account**.
* 3.1.3 Standings, matches, and brackets maintained through **FACEIT Bracket**.

### 3.2 Anti-Cheat

* 3.2.1 **FACEIT Anti-Cheat is mandatory** for all Crossfire matches.
* 3.2.2 Matches will not start if a player does not have anti-cheat enabled.
* 3.2.3 Players must run FACEIT AC during all matches. Failure results in **match forfeit**.
* 3.2.4 Players with **active VAC bans or platform bans** are ineligible.
* 3.2.5 If a player is banned mid-season, **all their team's results may be subject to forfeiture** at admin discretion.

### 3.3 Match Settings

* 3.3.1 **Freeze Time:** 15 seconds.
* 3.3.2 **Round Time:** 1:55 minutes.
* 3.3.3 **Regulation Start Money:** $800.
* 3.3.4 **Match Format:** MR12/3.
* 3.3.5 **Overtime:** MR6 with $12,500 start money, continue sides.

### 3.4 Map Veto

* 3.4.1 **BO3 Veto Format:** A removes → B removes → A picks → B picks → A removes → B removes → Decider.
* 3.4.2 Map vetoes occur immediately before matches.
* 3.4.3 **BO5 Grand Final Veto Format:** A removes → B removes → A picks → B picks → A picks → B picks → Remaining map.
* 3.4.4 In the Grand Final, the remaining unpicked map is awarded to the **Upper Bracket winner as its automatic map advantage**. This map is **not played**.
* 3.4.5 The Upper Bracket winner begins the Grand Final with a **1–0 series lead**. The four picked maps are played in the order selected, and the first team to reach **3 total map wins**, including the awarded map advantage, wins the Grand Final.

### 3.5 Server Location

* 3.5.1 All servers located in **North America (United States)**.
* 3.5.2 All players must be able to connect to North American servers with stable connection.

### 3.6 Camera Requirement

* 3.6.1 **VRS Integrity Rule:** Players must have a working camera setup available upon request.
* 3.6.2 Admins may request players to turn on cameras at any time during matches for integrity verification, especially in VRS-eligible matches.

## 4. Scheduling

### 4.1 Match Scheduling

* 4.1.1 **Default Match Day:** Middle day of each 3-day round window.
* 4.1.2 **Default Match Time:** 8:00 PM EST.
* 4.1.3 All times displayed in **Eastern Time (EST/EDT, as applicable)**.
* 4.1.4 Matches must be scheduled and played between **12:00 PM and 2:00 AM Eastern Time the following morning**, unless otherwise approved by Crossfire administration. This daily scheduling window does not extend any round deadline or the tournament's announced end date.

### 4.2 Reschedules

* 4.2.1 All reschedule requests must be sent via **match chat** in Discord server.
* 4.2.2 **Group Stage:** Matches can be rescheduled up until final day of group stage with mutual team agreement.
* 4.2.3 **Playoff Stage:** Reschedules permitted within round window with **admin approval**. WB Quarterfinals, WB Semifinals, and the WB Final carry extended reschedule windows through November 25, December 1, and December 7 respectively; see Section 2.3.8.

### 4.3 Punctuality and Forfeits

* 4.3.1 Teams must have **5 players on server within 15 minutes** of scheduled start time.
* 4.3.2 Failure to field 5 players results in **forfeit**.
* 4.3.3 **Forfeit Rule:** Any match not played within the scheduled round window without approved rescheduling will be recorded as a **forfeit loss**.

## 6. Broadcast and Media

### 6.1 Player Streaming

* 6.1.1 Players may stream their own POV of Crossfire GG Season 7 matches, subject to the requirements below and admin discretion.
* 6.1.2 **Stream Delay:** Player POV streams must have at least a **120-second (2-minute) delay**, unless Crossfire administration expressly approves a different delay in writing.
* 6.1.3 **Stream Title:** Players must include **@PlayCrossfire** in the stream title.
* 6.1.4 **VOD Settings:** Players streaming on Twitch must enable **Store past broadcasts** and **Always Publish VODs** under Settings → Stream in the Twitch dashboard. Streams on other platforms must make an equivalent full recording available to admins upon request.
* 6.1.5 Players should link their stream in the designated Discord self-promotion channel.
* 6.1.6 **Admin Discretion:** Crossfire administration may restrict, deny, or revoke POV streaming permission, require a longer delay, or approve exceptions to the streaming requirements for a specific match or broadcaster. Any exception requires express written admin approval through the Discord ticket system before it is used.
* 6.1.7 Streaming permission does not authorize a player, coach, or teammate to watch or receive information from any broadcast of their ongoing match; see Section 7.4.
* 6.1.8 Player POV streams are governed by this section. They do not require co-stream approval or a community caster role.

### 6.2 Community Broadcasting

* 6.2.1 A **community broadcast** is an independently produced broadcast using the in-game spectator feed (CSTV). Community broadcasts require **separate prior written approval from Crossfire administration**.
* 6.2.2 Tournament participation, access to a matchroom, possession of a CSTV connection, or co-stream approval does not automatically grant community broadcast permission.
* 6.2.3 Organizations and community casters must request approval and the community caster role through the official Discord ticket system or designated role-request channel before broadcasting.
* 6.2.4 Matches covered on an official Crossfire channel are reserved for the official broadcast unless Crossfire administration expressly approves additional coverage. Admins may approve exceptions, including partner broadcasts or coverage in another language, at their discretion.
* 6.2.5 Requests to broadcast in a language other than English must identify the language and receive approval before the broadcast begins.
* 6.2.6 Available matches and approved coverage will be communicated in the designated community caster channels. Approval applies only to the matches, channels, platforms, and conditions expressly authorized.
* 6.2.7 Community broadcasts must use at least a **120-second (2-minute) delay** unless Crossfire administration expressly approves a different delay in writing. Admins may impose additional title, attribution, recording, or coverage requirements as conditions of approval.
* 6.2.8 Crossfire administration may deny, limit, or revoke community broadcast permission at its discretion. Broadcasters must promptly comply with instructions to change or stop coverage.
* 6.2.9 Community caster approval does not authorize restreaming an official Crossfire or partner broadcast. Co-streaming requires approval under Section 6.5.

### 6.3 CSTV Access

* 6.3.1 To request CSTV access, contact Crossfire admins through the official Discord ticket system.
* 6.3.2 Approved broadcasters may receive access to designated broadcast channels where connection details are provided.
* 6.3.3 CSTV IPs, passwords, and other restricted connection details must not be shared with unauthorized parties without prior written admin approval.
* 6.3.4 CSTV access may be limited or revoked at admin discretion. Access does not authorize restreaming an official Crossfire or partner feed.
* 6.3.5 Co-streamers may not join the match server or use CSTV under a co-stream approval. Independently casting from CSTV requires separate community broadcast approval under Section 6.2.

### 6.4 Broadcast Rights

* 6.4.1 Official tournament matches may be broadcast by Crossfire staff or approved partners.
* 6.4.2 **Official Broadcast Delay:** Official broadcasts must use a minimum **90-second delay**, unless Crossfire administration expressly approves a different delay in writing. Any exception to a broadcast delay does not waive the prohibition on stream sniping.
* 6.4.3 Crossfire GG retains all broadcast rights for official tournament matches. Player POV, community broadcast, and co-stream permissions are limited permissions subject to this rulebook and any written approval conditions.
* 6.4.4 **Unauthorized Restreaming:** Rebroadcasting, simulcasting, or retransmitting an official Crossfire or approved partner broadcast, including its video, audio, commentary, or production feed, requires prior written approval from Crossfire administration.
* 6.4.5 Approved co-streams must follow Section 6.5. Community caster approval alone does not grant permission to co-stream or restream another broadcast.
* 6.4.6 Broadcast violations may result in a warning, required stream changes, termination of coverage, revocation of broadcast or CSTV access, or participant penalties under Section 7, depending on the circumstances and admin discretion.
* 6.4.7 Streaming Crossfire's official broadcast without approval is treated as an unauthorized rebroadcast. Crossfire will ask the streamer to stop and may submit a copyright takedown request to the platform if the streamer does not comply.

### 6.5 Co-Streaming Guidelines

#### 6.5.1 Overview

* Crossfire welcomes co-streams, but **every co-streamer must be approved before going live**.
* A **co-stream (watch party)** shows the official Crossfire broadcast on the co-streamer's channel with added live commentary, camera, or reactions.
* A **community broadcast** independently casts a match from CSTV with its own production. This requires separate written approval under Section 6.2 and is not covered by co-stream approval.
* **Player POV streams** follow Section 6.1 rather than these co-streaming guidelines.
* Streaming the official Crossfire broadcast without approval is an unauthorized rebroadcast of Crossfire's production.

#### 6.5.2 Applications and Approval

* Apply in the Crossfire Discord before co-streaming.
* Post in the designated co-stream application channel or open a ticket with:
  * Your channel link and streaming platform.
  * The matches or match days you want to co-stream.
  * Any regular co-hosts who will appear on your stream.
  * Your channel sponsors, if applicable.
* An admin will review your application and respond in Discord.
* Approved applicants receive the **Verified Co-Streamer** role. Do not go live with Crossfire broadcast content before receiving approval and this role.
* Approval is personal to you and your approved channel. It cannot be shared or transferred.
* Approval may cover a single match day or the entire season, as specified in the written approval message.

#### 6.5.3 Broadcast Requirements

* **Added Content:** You must add live commentary, a camera, or reactions. Showing the official feed without added content is a rebroadcast, not an approved co-stream.
* **Official Feed Only:** Use the official Crossfire broadcast as provided. Do not capture or re-encode the feed from another co-streamer's channel.
* **Visibility and Audio:** The Crossfire broadcast must remain visible on screen with its audio enabled throughout the broadcast, including breaks, sponsor segments, interviews, and the analyst desk.
* Your camera and overlays must not cover the scoreboard, team names, sponsor logos, or broadcast graphics.
* **Stay on the Event:** Do not switch to other games, other streams, or unrelated Just Chatting content while the Crossfire broadcast is live.
* **Stream Title:** Your title must start with **Crossfire Season 7 | Team A vs Team B (Co-stream)**, replacing Team A and Team B with the actual teams playing. Update the matchup when covering another match.
* **No In-Game Spectating:** Co-streamers may not join the match server or use CSTV for their co-stream. Independently casting from CSTV requires separate community broadcast approval.
* Follow the terms of service and applicable rules of your streaming platform.

#### 6.5.4 Sponsors

* Crossfire's sponsor placements must reach co-stream viewers as presented in the official broadcast.
* Do not cover, crop, blur, mute, skip, or talk over sponsor logos, ad reads, or sponsored segments.
* Do not mock or disparage Crossfire or its sponsors.
* Platform-native advertisements and your normal subscriptions, bits, and donations are allowed.
* Your own channel sponsors may appear on screen only if they:
  * Do not compete with a Crossfire sponsor.
  * Are not in a prohibited sponsor category.
  * Do not cover any part of the Crossfire broadcast.
* **Prohibited channel sponsor categories during a Crossfire co-stream:**
  * Gambling, sports betting, and esports betting.
  * Skin gambling, case opening, skin trading, and skin marketplace sites.
  * Alcohol, tobacco, vaping, and drugs.
  * Adult content.
  * Political advertising.
* If you are unsure whether a sponsor is allowed, obtain admin approval before going live.

#### 6.5.5 Conduct, Chat, and Guests

* Co-streamers represent the event while covering it. These conduct requirements also apply to their Discord and social posts about Crossfire.
* **Chat Moderation:** Co-streamers are responsible for removing hate speech, harassment, and spam aimed at players, casters, or admins.
* **No Harassment:** Criticism of gameplay and competitive decisions is permitted. Personal attacks, slurs, harassment, or targeting players are prohibited.
* **Guests:** Anyone appearing on your stream for more than a few minutes must be identified to an admin in advance. You are responsible for your guests' conduct and compliance with these rules.
* **No Players in Active Matches:** A player whose team still has an ongoing or upcoming match that match day may not appear on your co-stream. This restriction is intended to prevent ghosting.
* **No Claims of Official Status:** Do not describe your co-stream as "official" or imply that you represent Crossfire, its sponsors, Valve, or FACEIT.
* **No Banned Players:** Players banned by Crossfire, FACEIT, or Valve may not appear on your co-stream.

#### 6.5.6 VODs and Clips

* Your co-stream VOD may remain in your channel's native VOD archive.
* Do not upload the full co-stream to YouTube or another platform until **7 days after the match**. This restriction does not prohibit the approved live co-stream or its native VOD archive.
* Short clips **under 60 seconds** may be posted at any time if they credit Crossfire.
* By co-streaming, you permit Crossfire to use clips of your co-stream, including your voice and likeness, in Crossfire promotional content and highlight videos.
* Crossfire will obtain your permission before using your voice or likeness from the co-stream in paid advertisements.

#### 6.5.7 Platform Co-Stream Tools

* Use your platform's built-in co-stream feature when one is available for the approved broadcast and channel.
* Where the platform supports an official co-streamer list, Crossfire will add your approved channel to that list.
* Start your co-stream through the supported feature rather than capturing the feed yourself, where available, so viewership can be attributed to the event.
* If a supported feature is unavailable, obtain admin instructions on the approved method before going live.
* Platform rules continue to apply. Unauthorized rebroadcasting may result in a copyright complaint, takedown, or other platform enforcement.

#### 6.5.8 Stream Statistics

* Complete the **Crossfire co-stream stats form**, provided in the Crossfire Discord, after every match day you co-stream.
* Submit the form within **24 hours of the end of the broadcast**.
* Crossfire uses these statistics to report total event viewership to sponsors.
* Failure to submit the form counts as a **Strike 1 violation**. Any outstanding form must be submitted before your next approved match day.
* Repeated failures are subject to the escalating penalties below.

#### 6.5.9 Enforcement

Crossfire uses a **three-strike system per season**. Admins may proceed directly to removal for serious violations.

| Strike | Example Violation | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Incorrect title, overlay covering a broadcast graphic, or missing statistics form | Warning by DM. Live broadcast issues must be corrected within 10 minutes. Outstanding statistics forms must be submitted before the next approved match day. |
| 2 | Cutting away during a sponsor segment or ignoring a warning | Co-stream approval removed for the remainder of that match day. |
| 3 | A third violation during the season | Co-stream approval removed for the remainder of the season. |
| Immediate removal | Prohibited sponsor, intentionally covering a sponsor placement, harassment, ghosting, or no added commentary, camera, or reactions | Approval removed. Crossfire may report the stream or conduct to the platform, as applicable. |

* Unauthorized rebroadcasts are not eligible for the three-strike process. Crossfire will ask the streamer to stop and may submit a copyright takedown request if the streamer does not comply.
* Crossfire administration may update these guidelines or remove an approval at any time. Updates will be published in the rulebook or official Discord announcements.
* Questions and requests for exceptions must be directed to a Crossfire admin in Discord.

## 7. Penalties

* Cheating, account sharing, stream sniping, betting violations, and other integrity violations are subject to the penalties below, including potential disqualification and bans. Admins reserve final authority.

### 7.1 Cheating and Third-Party Software

* 7.1.1 **Cheating of any kind** results in immediate expulsion from Crossfire GG.
* 7.1.2 **Account sharing** during matches results in match forfeiture and expulsion.
* 7.1.3 If a player is banned mid-season, **all their team's results may be subject to forfeiture** at admin discretion, consistent with Section 3.2.5.
* 7.1.4 Use of unauthorized third-party programs prohibited.
* 7.1.5 **No custom player models** allowed.

### 7.2 Exploits and Illegal Actions

* 7.2.1 Exploiting known or unknown bugs for advantage is **prohibited**.
* 7.2.2 **Pixel walking, invisible ledges, or glitching** banned.
* 7.2.3 No bomb plants in **floating positions** or **through solid objects**.
* 7.2.4 Bombs must be **audible** to opposing team.
* 7.2.5 Players must not join enemy team during match.
* 7.2.6 Boosting involving **clipping is illegal**.
* 7.2.7 All infractions handled on **case-by-case basis**. Penalties can include forfeits or disqualification.

### 7.3 Communication Violations

* 7.3.1 Use of **racial slurs** results in **FFL (Forfeit Loss)**.
* 7.3.2 Second offense leads to **tournament ban**.
* 7.3.3 Public posting of disputes or admin conversations may result in penalties.
* 7.3.4 All players must uphold spirit of fair play and competition at all times.

### 7.4 Stream Sniping

* 7.4.1 Players and coaches may not watch any broadcast of their ongoing match, including Twitch streams, unless expressly permitted by Crossfire administration.
* 7.4.2 This restriction applies **between maps** and during **technical pauses**.
* 7.4.3 Admins will assess suspected violations and determine penalties **at their discretion**.
* 7.4.4 Violations **may result in a match forfeit and/or tournament ban**, at admin discretion.

### 7.5 Betting Violations

* 7.5.1 **Betting on own matches** strictly prohibited.
* 7.5.2 Violations result in immediate disqualification and potential permanent ban.

### 7.6 Repetitive Issues

* 7.6.1 Teams with repetitive rule violations subject to escalating penalties.
* 7.6.2 Admins reserve right to remove teams with habitual violations.

## 8. Tournament Administration

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do all players need a FACEIT account?**  
A: Yes. FACEIT AC is mandatory.

**Q: How does VRS affect seeding?**  
A: VRS-matched teams are seeded first, based on the fixed Valve Regional Standings publication dated October 5, 2026; the top 8 receive a bye directly to Playoffs. This is a seeding outcome of open registration, not an invitation.

**Q: How are teams without a VRS ranking seeded?**  
A: By North American league division in the order **ECL > Advanced > Main > Intermediate > Open9-10 > Open1-8 > New Team**, followed by league win/loss record and documented competitive results; see Section 2.4.

**Q: Can we change our substitutes during Playoffs?**  
A: Yes. Teams may make a maximum of **2 substitute roster changes total** across the Playoffs, limited to the **Sub 1 / Sub 2 slots** and subject to admin approval. Each replacement of one player counts as one change. Every lineup must still satisfy the applicable three-of-five requirement.

**Q: Does using an already registered substitute count toward the 2-change limit?**  
A: No. The limit applies to changes to the substitute roster slots, not the number of matches played by an already registered substitute. Every lineup must still satisfy the applicable three-of-five requirement. Substitutions during a match or between maps require a verifiable emergency and admin approval.

**Q: What counts as my team's "Core"?**  
A: For Non-VRS teams, the reference roster is their five original registered starters. For VRS-matched teams, it is the five players listed on their matched VRS roster in the **October 5, 2026 publication**. Teams must field at least three players from the applicable five for the entirety of every match, including Playoffs. The qualifying three may differ between matches.

**Q: How does the Grand Final veto work?**  
A: **Ban → Ban → Pick → Pick → Pick → Pick.** The remaining unpicked map is awarded to the Upper Bracket winner as its automatic map advantage and is not played. The Upper Bracket winner starts the series 1–0, and the four picked maps are played in selection order until a team reaches 3 total map wins.

**Q: Can I stream my POV?**  
A: Yes, subject to admin discretion, with at least a 2-minute delay, @PlayCrossfire in the title, and VODs enabled. Any exception requires prior written admin approval; see Section 6.1.

**Q: Can I community cast a match?**  
A: Community broadcasts from CSTV require separate prior written admin approval under Section 6.2. A co-stream approval does not grant CSTV or community broadcast permission.

**Q: Can I co-stream the official Crossfire broadcast?**  
A: Only after receiving written approval and the Verified Co-Streamer role. You must follow the co-streaming requirements in Section 6.5, including sponsor visibility, conduct, title, and statistics reporting requirements.

**Q: Can players or coaches watch their match's broadcast?**  
A: Players and coaches may not watch a broadcast of their ongoing match, including between maps or during technical pauses, unless expressly permitted by Crossfire administration. Violations may result in a match forfeit and/or tournament ban at admin discretion.

**Q: How do I contact admins or submit a protest?**  
A: Use the official Crossfire Discord ticket system. Formal protests must include supporting evidence and be submitted within 1 day of match conclusion.
